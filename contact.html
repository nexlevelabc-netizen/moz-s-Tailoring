Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. However, for wedding consultations or complex projects, we recommend calling ahead.

How long do alterations take?

Standard turnaround is 3-5 working days. Express service (24-48 hours) is available for a surcharge.

How much do alterations cost?

Prices vary based on the work needed. We provide free estimates - bring your garment in for a no-obligation quote.

Do you do repairs?

Yes, we handle all types of repairs including zips, tears, hems, and buttons. We'll assess if it's repairable when you bring it in.
