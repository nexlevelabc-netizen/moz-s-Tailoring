Frequently Asked Questions

How long do alterations take?

Most alterations are completed within 3-5 working days. Urgent services available for an additional fee.

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. However, calling ahead during peak times is recommended.

What should I bring?

Bring the garment(s) you want altered and, if possible, the shoes you plan to wear with them for accurate hemming.

Can you match fabrics/threads?

We have an extensive selection of threads and can usually match most fabrics. Bring a sample if you're concerned.
