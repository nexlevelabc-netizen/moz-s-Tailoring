Our Tailoring Services

Expert alterations and repairs for men, women, and wedding wear

Men's Tailoring

Complete suit alterations and adjustments for the perfect fit. We handle everything from simple hemming to complete suit restructuring.

What We Offer:

  • Suit alterations - Jacket, trousers, full re-fit
  • Shirt adjustments - Sleeve shortening, body taking in
  • Trouser alterations - Shortening, tapering, waist adjustment
  • Coat and jacket resizing
  • Blazer alterations
  • Formal wear tailoring

Last minute adjustments available for urgent needs.

Women's Alterations

Expert alterations for all types of women's clothing. From everyday wear to special occasion outfits.

What We Offer:

  • Dress resizing and reshaping
  • Skirt and trouser alterations
  • Zip replacements
  • Blouse and jacket fitting
  • Jeans hemming and tapering
  • Formal wear adjustments
  • Jacket and coat alterations

We work with all fabrics including delicate materials.

Wedding & Occasion Wear

Specialized alterations for your most important events. We handle wedding dresses with extra care and attention.

What We Offer:

  • Bridal gown alterations
  • Bridesmaid dresses
  • Prom dresses
  • Wedding suits
  • Evening gown adjustments
  • Mother of the bride/groom outfits
  • Page boy and flower girl outfits

Wedding consultations available by appointment.

Repairs

Quality repairs to extend the life of your favorite garments. We fix what others might deem unfixable.

What We Offer:

  • Tears and rips repair
  • Hem repairs and restoration
  • Button replacement
  • Zip repairs and replacements
  • Patch work
  • Reinforcement of worn areas
  • Lining replacement

We aim to repair rather than replace whenever possible.
